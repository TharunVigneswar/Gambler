# Gambler
A simple gambling themed website

You start with 10000 Neon Coins. You can gamble your way to get more coins.
<img width="959" height="410" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/72578382-8920-454a-8600-c53333fe43a7" />


You can choose between 4 different game modes - 
1. Coin Flip:
   You get double if you win or lose it all
   <img width="708" height="401" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8e1bc77e-dda5-4aca-bf63-e32e84eff972" />
2. Roulette:
   You can place your bets on a color - red, blac or green
   Red gives 2x
   Black gives 2x
   Green gives 14x
   <img width="581" height="410" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c55f4505-26cb-42b0-bf8b-9bd84b9cf9d0" />
3. Spin the wheel:
   You spend 500 NC per spin to get a chance of getting more rewards
   <img width="531" height="393" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4065b2de-1e9a-48e6-ab12-095f5942e1d8" />
4. Mystery box:
   This is a better version of the spin the wheel, you can spend 1000 per spin for better rewads like 10000 NC, 7500 NC, etc.
   <img width="539" height="410" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/857d8e15-a314-49ea-b488-2ded3d5ddb2f" />
   
Start gambling, enjoy your time
